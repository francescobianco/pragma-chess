Stockfish in Pragma Chess
=========================

Version: Stockfish @VERSION@

Pragma Chess includes Stockfish, a free and open source UCI chess engine, as
its default engine. It is a separate program: Pragma Chess starts it and talks
to it through the UCI protocol.

Stockfish is developed by the Stockfish developers (see AUTHORS) and licensed
under the GNU General Public License version 3 (see Copying.txt). Pragma Chess
itself is licensed under the MIT License.

This is the official release build "@ASSET@" of
https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/releases/tag/@TAG@
unmodified.

Source code: the complete corresponding source of this build, including the
neural network files it embeds, is attached to every Pragma Chess release as
stockfish-@TAG@-source.tar.gz
(https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/releases), and is also
available from https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/tree/@TAG@
