# Welcome window

`chess-study.png` is the picture on the left of the welcome window
(`dialogs/WelcomeDialog`): "Historic Chess Setup" from Stockcake
(stockcake.com), whose images are in the public domain (CC0), as PNG so no
image plugin is needed to read it.

Retouched: the slit of the bishop in the middle of the board, drawn ragged
in the original, is taken off (the 95 pixels of its mitre filled from the
wood around them).
